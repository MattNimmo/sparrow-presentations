# Impeccable Design Context — The Nest

## Design Context

### Users
Moving company employees at Sparrow Moving — movers, crew leads, sales reps, estimators, dispatchers. Used in **both contexts**: quick lookups on a phone in the field (gloves off between jobs, fast and direct) and at a desk during onboarding. New hires encounter it on day one. Veterans refer back when they need policy details or training refreshers.

### Brand Personality
**Grounded, energetic, straightforward.** Sparrow Moving was founded on caring for the individual — not corporate, not cold. The interface should feel like a well-organized crew room, not a compliance portal. Text speaks plainly. Design earns trust without trying too hard.

### Emotional Goal
*Part of something fresh.* New employees should open The Nest and feel they've joined a company that has its act together — modern, proud, and serious about their team. Not polished for polished's sake. Purposeful.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Light with dark mode toggle (system preference + manual)
- **Tone**: Confident but approachable. Clean without being sterile. The green brand color anchors everything — warm enough for a people business, vivid enough to feel energetic.
- **References**: Well-organized field guides, trade tool branding, clear operational signage. Think Patagonia's employee materials or a really good transit app.
- **Anti-references**: Generic HR portals, overly corporate intranets, glass-card dashboards.

### Design Principles
1. **Directness over decoration** — every element earns its place. No padding for padding's sake.
2. **Field-ready readability** — high contrast, clear hierarchy, usable one-handed on a phone
3. **Consistent rules everywhere** — no component behaves differently on different pages; the system is the design
4. **The brand color is the accent, not the wallpaper** — green signals importance; overuse dilutes it
5. **Dark mode is a first-class citizen** — not an afterthought; the same hierarchy and clarity must hold in both themes
