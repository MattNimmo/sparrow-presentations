Don't say
  • "I'll hold the date for you." We don't hold dates. The deposit locks it.
  • A tip amount before they ask. If they ask: around 15% of the total, split between the movers. Never expected, always appreciated.
  • Pounds per hour or truck cubic feet. Customers pay for time; inventory just sizes the crew.
  • "We have a military discount" or any discount. We don't run discounts.
  • "I'm still new." Say "I'll get you the exact answer from Matt within the hour," then follow up the same day.

Most asked

  1. Do you have my date open?
  2. What's my price, and what's included? (local quote review)
  3. Do you come out to give an estimate? How do you quote?
  4. Do you move on weekends? Why not?
  5. Can I send photos, a video or a list instead of a walkthrough?
  6. Do you do labor-only? Can you load or unload my U-Haul, pod or trailer?
  7. Do you have wardrobe, TV and picture boxes? What do they cost?
  8. Do you offer storage? Where, and what does it cost?
  9. How is your pricing figured: by the hour, by item or by weight?
  10. How do I book?
