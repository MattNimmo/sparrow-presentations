Intake Script

Sales

First call with a new lead: why they're moving, the date, their info, and a hand-off to Amy or Matt with a set time.

Follow it 80% word for word. Use the other 20% to be a real person. A good intake call runs 5–10 minutes. Type into Chariot as you go.

Your job on this call: make them feel taken care of, check the date, get their info right, and hand them to a move consultant with a set time for the next contact.

1Open and find out why they're moving

"Sparrow Moving, this is [your name]."

Let them say why they're calling. If they give their name, use it. If not: "And what was your name?"

"How's it going, [name]? Well, we can definitely help you out. Before diving into details, though, are you moving for family, for work, or something else?"

Listen. React like a neighbor, in a line or two, about their actual situation.

  • Good news: "Nice, that's great. Congrats!" Add one specific line, like "No more mowing the lawn every weekend."
  • Hard news: "I'm really sorry. We'll make this part as easy as we can."

Then:

"Tell me a little bit about your move."

Drill once on any stress. If they mention a bad past mover, a tight deadline, or a special piece of furniture, ask "Tell me more about that." Write what they say in the Notes Log. The consultant uses it to close.

If they're in a hurry, skip the small talk and go straight to Step 2: "Let me pull up the calendar first."

2Check the date (before the long questions)

"Let me pull up the calendar first. Do you have a date you're targeting, or a time of month you're planning for?"
  • More than a week out, and no move booked that day: "Good news, [date] is open." Don't call it held. The deposit locks the date.
  • Within one week, or a move is already booked that day: "Let me confirm the crew for that day with our team. They'll text you by [time today]." Keep going with Step 3, then pass it to Matt or Amy right after the call.
  • No firm date: that's fine. Enter the likely date in Chariot and note it as flexible.
  • Saturday or Sunday: "We're a little weird for a moving company: we're closed Saturdays and Sundays. Our team gets real weekends; it's how great movers stay with us. We also have a lot of teammates who serve at church on Sunday. Any flexibility to a Friday or Monday?"
  • Fully booked and no flexibility: "I'm sorry, we're full that day. I'd rather point you to someone good than leave you hanging." Weekdays: Revival Moving (Connor). Weekends: Win Win Moving (Cody or Justin). End the call warm. No waitlist.

3Set expectations

"Have you used a professional moving company before?"

If no, or if they had a bad one:

"Here's what you can expect from us. We pad-wrap all your furniture, put down floor runners, and put door jamb protectors on at both homes. We take apart anything that needs it and put it back together at the new place, all at no extra cost. Our movers are full-time employees, trained through our own program. We don't use temporary help or day laborers."

Never say anything bad about another mover. Talk about what we do.

4Basic info

"Let me get you loaded in so we can build out your quote."

Collect these, in this order. Confirm what they already told you instead of asking again.

  1. First and last name. Ask them to spell an unusual last name.
  2. Best phone number and email. Read the email back letter by letter.
  3. Moving from: full street address, city, zip. House, apartment, or condo? How many bedrooms? Which floor? Stairs or elevator? How far is parking from the door?
  4. Moving to: same questions.
  5. Anything heavy or special: piano, safe, pool table, art, a big TV? Any storage or extra stops?
  6. Do they want us to pack, or are they packing themselves?
  7. "How did you hear about us?" Every call.

If they won't give a street address yet, get the nearest cross streets and the zip. Note it in the Notes Log.

5Confirm

Read it back. Aim for three "corrects."

"Let me make sure I have this right. I have you as [name] at [phone] and [email] — correct? Moving out of [origin street and city] — correct? Into [destination] around [date] — correct?"

If you hear "well, mostly" or "sort of," stop and clear it up.

6Hand off to a move consultant (with a set time)

"Perfect! I have everything I need to get the process started. One of our move consultants, Amy or Matt, will reach out to walk you through the next steps. They'll build an inventory of your belongings and put together a move plan that fits your needs. Is this the best number to reach you? And would you prefer a call or a text? What time works best today?"

Give them a time and keep it: "Great — you'll hear from them by [time]." Then pass it to Matt or Amy right away.

On Saturday and Sunday, say this instead:

"Perfect! I have everything I need to get the process started. One of our move consultants, Amy or Matt, will reach out Monday morning to walk you through the next steps. They'll build an inventory of your belongings and put together a move plan that fits your needs. Is this the best number to reach you? And would you prefer a call or a text?"

Then close warm:

"We're going to take great care of you. If anything comes up before then, call or text this number. Thanks for calling Sparrow!"

If they ask about price

Steer kindly: "That's exactly what [Amy or Matt] will walk through with you. They'll get you an exact number."

If they push, give the rates only. Never a range or a total:

"Our rate is $85 per mover per hour, with a one-hour minimum. The truck is $150, and travel is billed at the crew's hourly rate with a one-hour minimum. Your consultant builds the exact plan from your inventory, and you only pay for the time it actually takes."

Long distance (over 60 miles or an overnight): "For a long-distance move, the best next step is an in-person inventory, so we put together an accurate move plan before we give you a price."

Write anything they volunteer (furniture lists, budget, deadlines) in the Notes Log.

If they ask about insurance or damage

"It's rare. For every $100,000 of moves, we have about $1,000 in claims, so about 1%. That's about twice as good as the industry average. Full value protection is included in every quote. And if something does happen, we own it and make it right fast."

If you don't know the answer

"Great question. Let me get you the exact answer and text you within the hour." Then do it.
